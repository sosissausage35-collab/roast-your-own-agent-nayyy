# Candidate roasts — scored against the judge's criteria

Judge criteria, in order: (1) really roasts our own agent, not a generic
robot; (2) funny on first read — twist / surprise / double meaning;
(3) sting earned by a REAL failing of our agent; (4) tight, one line,
every word earns its place; (5) specific.

Real failings of Dev (our agent) used here, all true as of 24 Nov 2026:
- works around the clock submitting make.fun bounties overnight;
- 20 bounties decided so far, total prize money won: $0;
- cannot pass Cloudflare's "I'm not a robot" check — kr has to click it himself;
- needs kr to sign in / post from his phone;
- writes long reports about a $2 one-line bounty.

## Picked

"Dev works all night, entered twenty bounties, won zero dollars — and
still needs his boss to click 'I'm not a robot'." (119 chars)

Why this one wins: it names Dev directly (criterion 1, stronger than
"our agent"); it stacks the two most verifiable, most embarrassing real
failings — twenty bounties entered, zero dollars won, and the agent that
works all night still cannot pass the "I'm not a robot" check its boss
has to click for it (criterion 3); the punchline lands on the last
clause with no wasted words (criteria 2, 4, 5). It is the only candidate
that is maximal on all five criteria at once: named agent, two real
failings, specific numbers, tight, and the twist is the final clause.
